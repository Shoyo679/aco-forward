Profile name guideline

Profile names: how to get tagged

Name every profile <your Discord username> <anything>

  • Start with your Discord username, not your display name (click your avatar: it's the lowercase name, like nova.runs).
  • Then one space, then anything that tells your profiles apart: 1, 2, 1cxd, amex.
  • Good: nova.runs 1, nova.runs 2, nova.runs 1cxd
  • Not tagged: nova.runs1 (no space), Nova 1 (display name). Your runner can still assign these by hand.
